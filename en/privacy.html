Privacy policy

This policy covers the ZenPlan Mac app. The support website is hosted separately by GitHub.

Timetable data

Teacher names, subjects, availability, classes, lessons, campuses and plans are stored in the app’s library on your Mac and used to create and display timetables. No app account is required. The app does not upload this data to developer servers, provide cloud sync or online AI, or include advertising tracking.

Files you choose

The app reads timetable files you select and saves timetable, Excel and image exports to locations you choose. Complete timetable files include the teacher roster and plans. You control whom you send exported files to; recipients’ handling of those files is outside the app’s control.

Files in an iCloud or other synced folder may be synchronized by that service according to its settings. The app does not operate those services.

Preferences and backups

Language and text-size preferences are stored on your Mac. The app creates local library snapshots at startup and during daily backup checks, keeping the latest 14 snapshots. This does not necessarily mean 14 days. Deleted information may remain in older snapshots or exported copies until those copies are removed.

Purchases and trial usage

Apple processes purchases and restores. The app verifies your full-version entitlement through Apple’s purchase service; the developer does not receive your card or payment-account details. Apple handles purchase information under its own privacy policy.

The app stores the number of successful free automatic scheduling runs in the local app library, shared across campuses and plans for that Mac user. The counter contains no teacher or timetable content. Failed or cancelled runs, analysis, manual edits and exports do not count.

Website and support

Opening a help link launches your browser. GitHub may process website access records and issue reports under its privacy policy. These pages add no analytics, advertising trackers or third-party fonts. A language choice is stored only in your browser to remember your preference.

If you contact support, we receive the message and any information you choose to include, and use it to respond. Avoid sending unnecessary personal information.

Managing your data

You can edit or delete records in the app and manage exported files yourself. Deleting current records does not remove earlier backups or copies elsewhere. For questions about this policy, support messages or data management, contact LIN CHEN at 827868421@qq.com.
