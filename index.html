Support

Plan school timetables, manage teachers and campuses, and export schedules on your Mac. No app account is required.

Free trial and full version

You get 20 successful automatic scheduling runs per Mac user’s app library, shared across campuses and plans. Failed or cancelled runs, feasibility analysis, manual editing and exports do not use your allowance. A one-time in-app purchase unlocks unlimited automatic scheduling; there is no subscription. Viewing, editing, saving and exporting remain available after the trial.

Open Settings → Full version to buy or restore a purchase. The price shown comes from your App Store region. To restore, use the Apple Account used for the original purchase.

Save and back up

Changes are saved in the app’s local library. Regularly use File → Save timetable file to keep your own complete .zzgkpk backup. Excel and image exports are for viewing and sharing; they do not replace a complete backup.

Opening a timetable file replaces the current campus workspace and the shared teacher roster. Save existing work first and read the confirmation message.

Scheduling

Check teacher availability, enabled periods, lesson counts and combined-class settings. If lessons remain unscheduled, review the feasibility analysis. You can cancel a run and adjust the settings.

Get help

Email 827868421@qq.com or use GitHub Issues. Include the app and macOS versions, Mac chip type, steps to reproduce, and expected and actual results.

Do not post real teacher names, contact details or timetable files containing personal information in public issues. Use fictional data in examples. Support messages you send are used to address your request.
